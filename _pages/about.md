<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I am an undergraduate student in **Artificial Intelligence at [Tianjin University](https://www.tju.edu.cn/)** (2023.09 - present). My research interests include **multimodal large language models**, **embodied AI**, **vision-language-action models**, grounded vision-language reasoning, and reinforcement learning.

My current work studies how multimodal models can reason about the world, learn from counterfactual visual feedback, and act efficiently in embodied environments. I enjoy taking an idea from problem formulation and method design through implementation, evaluation, and paper writing.

I am open to research discussions and collaborations. You can reach me at [3023001457@tju.edu.cn](mailto:3023001457@tju.edu.cn), or download my [resume (PDF)](/images/feng-jiahe-resume.pdf). You can also find my publications and citation profile on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QCKzWl0AAAAJ).
